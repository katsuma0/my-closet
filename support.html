My Closet

My Closet turns photos of your clothes into stickers and lets you put them together into looks. Everything stays on your device.

Adding clothes

Tap + and choose Take photo or Choose photos. For the cleanest cut-out, lay the item flat on a plain sheet or hang it against a plain wall, in soft, even light, with the whole item in the picture. If a bit of background is left, open the item, tap Edit, and use Remove; if part of the item went missing, use Add back.

Looks and Mix

New look (under +) opens a board to arrange items on. Mix shows a row for each kind of item: swipe the rows, then Save look. Long-press nearly anything for more options.

Thrifted, favourites and wear counts

Turn on Thrifted when you add or edit an item, or choose several items with Select and tap Thrifted. More shows how many are thrifted, your favourites, and what you wear most.

Backups and moving to a new phone

Your closet is stored only inside the app on this device, so keep a backup: More, then Backup, and save the file somewhere safe, such as iCloud Drive. On the new phone, install the app and use More, then Restore a backup. Deleting the app deletes its data.

Privacy

The app collects no data and makes no network connections. Read the privacy policy.
