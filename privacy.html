Privacy Policy

My Closet · Updated November 2026

My Closet does not collect any data about you. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and the app does not connect to the internet.

What stays on your device

The photos you add, the cut-out items made from them, your looks, names, brands, notes, prices and wear counts are stored only on your device, inside the app. They are not sent anywhere, and nobody else, including the developer, can see them.

Camera and photos

The camera and your photo library are used only when you choose to add a picture of your clothes. The background is removed on the device; nothing is uploaded. A picture of a look is saved to Photos only when you ask for it.

Backups

A backup file is made only when you tap Backup in the app, and you choose where it goes. Restore reads only the file you pick.

Deleting your data

  • In the app, More, then Clear everything, deletes every item and look.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything in it.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, this page will be updated first, with a new date at the top.
