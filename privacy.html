Privacy Policy

My Closet · Last updated November 2026

My Closet does not collect, share or sell any personal information.

Your data

Everything you add, including photos, items, looks and notes, is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Camera and photos

The app uses the camera and photo library only when you choose to add a photo or save a look. Photos are processed entirely on your device.

No tracking

The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking, and does not connect to the internet.

Deleting your data

You can delete all of your data at any time with More > Clear everything, or by deleting the app.

Changes

Any updates to this policy will be posted on this page.

Questions: see the support page.
