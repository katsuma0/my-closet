# my closet

A calm, sticker-style wardrobe app: photograph your clothes, they become die-cut stickers, then put them together into looks. Everything stays on the phone.

- **Closet**: upload or snap a photo; the background is removed on-device and the item gets a smooth sticker border. Sort into Tops / Outerwear / Bottoms / Shoes / Accessories and sub-types (or add your own). Each item's card takes a soft tint of its colours, and its outline a colour of its own.
- **Getting started**: an empty closet opens on *Take photo* / *Choose photos*. Before the camera or the photos open, a short *Photo tips* page shows how to get a clean cut-out (a light item on a dark background, a dark item on a light one, soft light, the whole item in the photo), with *Take photo* and *Choose photos* (which offers the photo library, the camera or files); *Don't show this again* skips it, and More > Photo tips brings it back. A new item only needs its category, which is pre-picked from the item's shape; type, brand, name, notes and price are tucked away. A *Thrifted* switch sits right under the category. An item left unnamed is named plainly from its brand and type ("Nike T-shirt", with the brand's first letter in capitals), or its kind ("Top"); a name you type is kept. *Add and do another* saves it and opens the camera or photos again straight away. While a photo is read and its background removed, a small card in the middle of the screen says so ("Getting your photo ready", then "Removing the background").
- **Brands**: type a brand once and it is kept in a list; every item's Brand menu offers it after that, with *Add a brand…* for new ones. Brands can be searched and filtered on.
- **Your own order**: long-press an item and choose *Move items* (or a look and *Move looks*): hold a card a moment and drag it to its place, then *Done*. The page then sorts by *My order* (also in Sort and filter). With a category, filter or search on, the items shown swap places among themselves.
- **Select**: *Select* in the closet's header (or *Select* in an item's long-press menu) picks several items at once: edit them together (category, type, brand), favourite them, mark them *Thrifted* (or take the mark off) or delete them.
- **Bulk add**: choose up to 40 photos at once and they are all cut out together, each with its category guessed from its shape (one tap fixes a wrong guess, or "set all"). Tap any photo to fix its cut-out with the same tools as the one-item form, and optionally use that strength for every photo.
- **Background removal**: works on an item lying on a sheet in an ordinary room: the wall, floor, furniture and rug around the sheet are removed with the sheet, leaving only the item with a smooth edge. Long thin background lines (creases, floorboard gaps, shadow edges) are dropped instead of leaving spikes, and the crop is tight. The cut edge is shaved by a pixel or two and its colours are taken from just inside, so no line of wall or sheet shows between the item and its outline (thin straps and laces are left whole).
- **Fixing a cut-out**: a *Less / More* slider for how much background goes; *Remove* (tap anything left over; a tap just beside a thin line still catches it) and *Add back* (tap a part of the item that was cut away; the removed parts show faintly so you can find them). Pinch the photo to zoom in for the small bits, drag to move around, *Fit* to zoom out. *Undo* takes back the last tap.
- **Create**: the + in the middle of the tab bar opens a Create sheet with big tiles: *Take photo*, *Choose photos* (one or many), *New look* and *Surprise me*. Hold the + for a quick menu.
- **Mix**: a dress-up screen, one row per kind. Swipe a row, or tap the item next to the middle, to change it; the item in the middle is the one chosen. Rows loop round: the empty spot (nothing chosen) sits between the last item and the first, so you can keep swiping either way. *Rows* chooses what Mix shows: tap a row to pick its kind right there, drag the handle to reorder, minus to remove a row, *Add a row* for up to ten, and a kind can have more than one row (two tops for a shirt under a sweater). The rows keep a comfortable size and scroll, while *Rows*, *Shuffle* and *Save look* stay put above the tab bar. *Shuffle* spins every row to a fit whose colours go together. *Save look* turns the selection into a look on the Looks page, already laid out.
- **Looks**: a mood-board collage: drag an item with one finger; put a second finger down anywhere (even on another item) to pinch and turn the same item. Tap an item and the bar below offers *To front*, *To back*, *Swap* (other items of the same kind in its exact place), *Flip* and *Delete*; tap the board to go back to *Add*, *Background*, *Arrange* and *Worn*. *Background* opens a strip of board colours, led by *Auto*: the board takes a soft tint of the look's own colours and each item's outline takes the item's main (or second) colour. *Arrange* hops the items into a new layout each time (a column, a zigzag, a lean or a loose scatter, with tops high, bottoms under them, shoes at the foot and accessories around the edges). An empty look is only kept once it has a name, and a look without a name shows no name on its card. The board follows light and dark like the closet's tiles: *Auto* takes the dark tint of the look's colours in dark, and each board colour has a dark version. A look's card shows the whole look on its board, zoomed in so the items fill the card to a thin margin: an item that hangs over the board's edge in the editor is shown whole, never cut off with a straight edge. Hearts show only on favourite looks, straight on the board (favourite a look from its long-press menu).
- **Worn counter**: inside a look, *Worn* counts how often you wore it, with minus and plus (and *Undo* after a change). Each look card shows the count.
- **More**: counts per category, looks, favourites, thrifted items, the most worn items and look, and the closet's value (tap it for the price list). Settings live here too: appearance (auto, light, dark), *Types* (tap a type to show or hide it in each category and add your own; *Select* picks several to show, hide or delete, built-in ones too, and *Reset* brings the built-in ones back), *Mix rows*, backup, restore and clear, and *Privacy* (the privacy policy, in the app).
- **Search, favourites and filters**: the closet and the looks each have a search box (name, type, brand, notes, category, colour or "thrifted"; every word has to match, "black boots"), a heart button that shows favourites only, and a filter button: sort by newest, oldest, colour (rainbow order), name, most worn or least worn, and filter by colour family, brand, or not worn lately / never worn. The sort is remembered.
- **Suggested looks**: "Style" on an item, "Surprise me" and Mix's *Shuffle* pick one item per kind whose colours go together, leaning toward items that have rested, are in no look yet or are favourites, and never repeating the last few suggestions or an existing look.
- **Share**: long-press a look and choose Share to send it as a picture, or save the picture where sharing is not available.
- **iPad**: three columns (four on a wide landscape iPad), a wider page, and sheets that float as cards. Drag photos in from Photos, Files or Safari (Split View) to add them. With a keyboard: Cmd-N new item, Cmd-Shift-N new look, Cmd-F search, Cmd-1 to Cmd-4 for Closet, Mix, Looks and More, Escape to close.
- **Shortcuts & long-press**: long-press the app icon for Add clothes, Create a look, Mix and More; long-press nearly anything inside (items, looks, tabs, the + in the middle of the tab bar, board items) for quick actions. Haptics are the phone's own in the iOS app.

Photo tip: lay the item flat on a light sheet or plain surface, or hang it against a plain wall, in soft, even light, with the whole item in the photo. *Remove* and *Add back* fix what is left.

## iPhone app

`ios/` holds a ready-made Xcode project that wraps this web app: open `ios/Closet.xcodeproj`, pick your team, press Run. Inside the app the bottom bar is the native iOS tab bar (Closet, Mix, Looks, More) with a native + menu (Add clothes, Create a look, Surprise me); it steps aside while a sheet is open and follows the app's light or dark choice. The app has a privacy manifest (no data collected, no tracking) and runs entirely from the bundle. `privacy.html` and `support.html` are the privacy policy and support pages the App Store asks for (publish them with GitHub Pages). See `ios/README.md`, which also lists what to check before sending it to the App Store.

## Run it

It's a static web app (no build step). Serve the folder and open it on your phone:

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8080
```

Or enable GitHub Pages for the repo. On iPhone, open in Safari → Share → *Add to Home Screen* for a full-screen app. Everything is stored privately on the device (IndexedDB); use More → Backup to export.

The serif typeface, Cormorant Garamond, is bundled in `css/fonts` under the SIL Open Font License (`css/fonts/OFL.txt`).
